# Generated visual direction

Original bitmap artwork was generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Astro processes source PNG files into responsive WebP delivery. Vector interface diagrams and the logo are authored separately in SVG/CSS.

+## Brand identity concept

Use case: logo-brand. Create a premium 3 by 3 brand identity overview board for fictitious B2B cybersecurity SaaS Tracegate. Landscape 16:10 presentation board, crisp Swiss-inspired agency design. Brand meaning: connected attack paths interrupted by a deliberate security boundary. Reduced geometric mark: two parallel vertical bracket/gate strokes intersected by a diagonal signal path; simple repeatable orange mark, not shield or padlock. Colors ink #171B20, soft white #F6F7F8, signal orange #F2693A, silver #DDE1E5. Typography geometric sans similar Geist, mono details. Nine panels with strong gutters and disciplined negative space: 1 large Tracegate wordmark and gate mark; 2 construction geometry; 3 tiny refined cloud security interface mockup; 4 tagline exactly 'See the path. Stop the breach.'; 5 palette chips labeled Ink, Signal, Cloud; 6 typography Aa and numerical specimen; 7 matte white stationery with orange gate; 8 cinematic abstract brushed metal gate with orange light and off-white background; 9 precise UI chips and geometric pattern. Charcoal outer canvas, mostly light panels. Sparse text, readable, no purple, no gradients behind text, no fake customer logos. Sophisticated security brand, technical and calm.

## Original boundary study

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium cybersecurity SaaS website hero artwork. Landscape 4:3 crop. A clean high-end 3D render of two tall vertical brushed aluminum gate slabs, one foreground left and one rear right, precisely separated by a gap. Fine signal-orange diagonal light path enters from lower left and is interrupted in the gap, a single orange reflection along one edge. Subtle beveled edges, precise geometry, beautiful tactile metallic surfaces. A few thin orthogonal pale gray diagram connector lines on ground plane imply connected infrastructure. On a very light cool gray almost white background, atmospheric studio lighting, soft accurate shadows. Sophisticated technical art direction, calm futuristic architecture, generous blank space around object. No text, no letters, no labels, no shields, no padlocks, no humans, no neon outer glow, no blue, no purple, no dark background. Object centered right, camera orthographic slightly elevated, considered editorial composition.

## Connected-system campaign artwork

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: original premium cybersecurity SaaS hero artwork for Tracegate, suitable for a high-end website like Wiz or Cyberhaven, not a template. Create a highly art-directed 3D isometric sculptural cloud ecosystem on a warm pale peach seamless background. Landscape 4:3. Composition: a large beautiful translucent frosted glass angular gateway/portal in center with a saturated coral-orange metal frame, diagonally oriented. The frame is a reduced gate metaphor, open rectangular architectural structure. A striking continuous glossy orange cable enters from front left, crosses through the portal, and branches into thin orange wires connecting six raised miniature infrastructure blocks: charcoal server tower, translucent glass data cylinder, rounded cloud-shaped stone sculpture, tiny brushed silver cubes and a black faceted identity token. Many fine connecting paths and beautiful layered glass panels create a connected risk ecosystem with visual depth. High quality tactile physical rendering, soft ambient occlusion, sharp material details, sophisticated warm lighting. Low elevation orthographic camera. Main mass upper right and center, cables extending into lower left. Deliberate negative space at perimeter. Palette coral #f2693a, pale peach #f9e4d8, graphite #171b20, silver, frosted clear glass. Visually expressive, memorable, playful but precise enterprise product brand. No text, no numbers, no logos, no labels, no shield or lock, no people, no blue or purple, no neon glow, no dark background. A coherent original scene, not disconnected floating shapes.
