# Tracegate product context

Tracegate is a fictional cybersecurity SaaS brand and Astro marketing portfolio. It demonstrates connected cloud posture, identity permissions, and potential attack paths. The promise is “Turn connected risk into a clear next step.”

The audience is cloud security, identity, security operations, and platform engineering teams at growing SaaS organizations, plus portfolio reviewers. Visitors should understand the relationship model, inspect synthetic evidence, compare capabilities, and explore a self-guided walkthrough.

The voice is clear, calm, technical, and decisive. Explain the mechanism, respect the operator, and earn every claim. A potential path is a question to review, not evidence of a breach. Human approval, dependency checks, testing, and verification remain part of any proposed intervention.

All resources, principals, prices, organizations, and product behaviors are illustrative. There are no real customers, certifications, operational connectors, policy evaluators, account connections, or verified performance results. Forms preview information locally and never send or persist it.

The incumbent brand identity is preserved: gate mark, Plex typography, orange signals, neutral surfaces, authored security diagrams, ordinary scrolling, light and dark themes. Root DESIGN.md describes its implementation. Source truth: src/data/brand.json, docs/BRAND.md, README.md, and the content JSON files.
